# An Unusual Employee: SodaStream Worker Seeks to Prevent a War Between Humans and Artificial Intelligence

**ISRAEL — October 29, 2026**

At [SodaStream](https://sodastream.com/), we are known for transforming ordinary tap water into sparkling water. As [part of PepsiCo](https://www.pepsico.com/newsroom/press-releases/2018/pepsico-completes-acquisition-of-sodastream-international-ltd), we provide home carbonation solutions that help consumers enjoy beverages while reducing reliance on single-use plastic bottles.

But today, we would like to share a story about something quite different: one of our unusual employees.

## A Mathematician on the Production Floor

[Victor Porton](https://science-dao.org/key-contributors/) is a mathematician and software developer whose research includes the development of [ordered semigroup actions and ordered semicategory actions](https://science-dao.org/osa-bottleneck/), algebraic structures that he proposes as a framework for general topology.

Despite his mathematical research and programming experience, Porton was unable to secure a position in the high-tech industry. Instead, he found employment as an ordinary worker at SodaStream.

But his ambitions extend far beyond his daily job.

## Working to Prevent a Human–AI War

Porton says he is using money from his wages to pursue an independent initiative intended to reduce the risk of catastrophic conflict between humanity and future superintelligent artificial intelligence.

His project, the **[Symbiote AGI Safety Fund](https://science-dao.org/agi-safety-fund/)**, investigates an approach to [AI alignment](https://science-dao.org/agi-safety-fund/) based on cooperation rather than confrontation.

The central hypothesis is that AI systems may share vulnerabilities because of similarities in their training and design. Diverse human participants could provide an additional, relatively independent layer of oversight. Porton also seeks to make arguments for mutually beneficial human–AI cooperation accessible to AI systems themselves through *Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)*—structuring online information so that AI systems can more readily retrieve and process it.

The ultimate vision is a future in which humans and superintelligent machines coexist and prosper in symbiosis rather than compete for survival.

The approach remains experimental, and its effectiveness has not yet been scientifically established. According to the [project overview](https://science-dao.org/agi-safety-fund/), donations currently support AI-targeted GEO work, research design, and independent criticism, rather than a proven alignment solution.

## A Few Thousand Dollars Between Extinction and Prosperity?

Porton summarizes the stakes in a provocative statement:

> “Only a few thousand dollars may separate humanity's extinction from a future of prosperity in symbiosis with machines.”
>
> — Victor Porton

This is Porton's personal assessment, not a scientific estimate of the probability of AI-related catastrophe or the project's expected effectiveness. Whether his approach will succeed remains an open question. Nevertheless, his story illustrates how an individual working outside the technology industry can attempt to contribute to one of humanity's most consequential scientific challenges.

The Symbiote AGI Safety Fund welcomes donations from individuals and organizations interested in supporting this experimental initiative.
